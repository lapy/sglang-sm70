# sglang-sm70

SM70 (V100 / Volta) support for SGLang: Volta kernels, attention backends and serving recipes for 32 GB
V100s, carried as ten slices on upstream `sgl-project/sglang` `2c5b680aa6` (`main` carries the whole
stack; slice PRs `#21` and `#2`–`#10`, all merged). Layout: [`docs/v100/STACK.md`](docs/v100/STACK.md).
Docs index: [`docs/v100/README.md`](docs/v100/README.md).
Credits, including the predecessor fork this stack was assembled and rebased from: [`NOTICE`](NOTICE).

The serve path is validated end to end on `Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-NVFP4`, TP4, 4x V100-SXM2-32GB:

| check | result |
|---|---|
| raw completion `The capital of France is` | ` Paris. Paris is a city...` (matches the predecessor fork's reference) |
| raw completion `2 + 2 =` | ` 4` |
| raw completion `def fibonacci(n):` | correct `if n <= 1` body |
| chat + reasoning | answers correctly, reasoning content present |
| tool call | `get_weather {"city": "Dublin"}`, `finish_reason: tool_calls` |
| streaming | works |
| 2k-context question | answers correctly |
| decode throughput | 108.2 tok/s (two-point, MTP on) |

The earlier degenerate output (prompt echo, then zeros) was a **missing dense marlin_v100 repack**: the
loader globs `_sm70_marlin_v100_dense*.so`, found nothing, and fell back to SGLang's in-tree repack, an
intentional zero-output stub below sm_80. Both extensions are now installed under the exact names the
loaders glob and verified through the loader, which fails loudly when either is absent. The model
serves correctly; the Qwen4 forward path was never the problem, and no comparison against the
predecessor fork is outstanding.

## Performance

Tuning campaign, measurements and roadmap live under `docs/v100/`:

- [`PERFORMANCE-TUNING.md`](docs/v100/PERFORMANCE-TUNING.md) — what we changed, measured before/after,
  what was measured and rejected, the 150 W / 135 MHz clock caveat, and the structural roadmap.
- [`perf/forks-measured-results.md`](docs/v100/perf/forks-measured-results.md) — every measured number
  found in the predecessor fork's and sglang-V100's docs and benchmarks, with citations.
- [`perf/knob-inventory.md`](docs/v100/perf/knob-inventory.md) — every SM70 tunable, its default, effect
  and how to A/B it.
- [`perf/architecture-analysis.md`](docs/v100/perf/architecture-analysis.md) — where the 26 ms MTP step
  actually goes (latency-bound: 7.6 ms of memory traffic, ~18 ms of launch/collective latency).

Headline, measured 2026-11-11 on 4x V100 at a 150 W cap: single-stream **108.2 tok/s** decode
(two-point, median of five; server-reported 110.2), acceptance 2.81 of 4 drafts, clocks at 1530 MHz,
TTFT 387 ms. Aggregate **75 / 119 / 182 / 198 tok/s** at 1/2/4/8 streams: parity with the predecessor
fork on single stream, ahead on aggregate.

## Open items

Correctness is closed for Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-NVFP4. What remains is the performance roadmap in
`PERFORMANCE-TUNING.md`:

- **FR-Spec hot-vocab draft head** — shard the 248k-vocab draft LM head (three passes per iteration,
  ~20% of all traffic) so a wider draft window becomes affordable.
- **Single-graph MTP iteration** — target-verify, draft-decode and draft-extend are three graph
  replays today, with most of the step in launch and collective latency.
- **Sharded HC mixers** — 1266 MB/step of hyper-connection `nn.Linear`s is replicated outside the TP
  plan.

Two caveats apply to every number above:

- these were taken at a 150 W cap with the cards at 1530 MHz (135 MHz when idle), so a cold first
  request measures the clock ramp, not the engine; absolute tok/s are not comparable with 300 W cards.
- `SGLANG_SM70_MTP_LOCAL_TOP1` deadlocks the scheduler (watchdog, no progress on any request); leave
  it off.

## Environment pitfalls

Install sequence: `scripts/install_v100.sh`, explained step by step in
[`docs/v100/INSTALL.md`](docs/v100/INSTALL.md). These cost the most time:

- `sglang-kernel` must be the torch-matching build; source builds need
  `-DSGL_KERNEL_V100_ONLY=ON` (38 objects, ~11 minutes) — without it the sm90a FA3 kernels are
  compiled too and the build takes an hour.
- No `nvidia-nccl-cu13` in the venv (it shadows torch's cu12 NCCL: "named symbol not found").
- `marlin_v100` needs `MARLIN_V100_SKIP_BF16_COMPAT=1` under CUDA 12.6.
- `--linear-attn-decode-backend tilelang`: Triton lowers `tanh` to PTX `tanh.approx`, sm_75+.
- FlashInfer must be the pinned revision **with** `patches/flashinfer-sm70.patch`, and the JIT
  sampling call must match its `DeviceSamplingFromProb` signature (7 args + `DETERMINISTIC`).
- PLE embedding offload is mandatory on 4x32 GB; on-device OOMs.
