## Deployment status (4x V100, 2026-11-10)

The stack **boots and serves** `Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-NVFP4` (TP4): 128 GB loaded at 19.89 GB/rank,
target-verify and draft-decode CUDA graphs captured, QSA kernels JIT-compiled, `/health` 200,
**84 tok/s decode** (`/generate`, 128 tokens, ignore_eos).

Correctness is not there yet: generations are degenerate (prompt echo, then zeros). Everything
environmental is fixed and every opt-in SM70 kernel has been ruled out — see
[issue #14](https://github.com/lapy/sglang-sm70/issues/14). The remaining suspect is the merge of
the SM70 port into upstream's newer Qwen4 forward path (`srt/models/qwen4_exp.py`,
`srt/layers/hyperconnection.py`), so the next step is a hunk-by-hunk comparison against
`sglang-sxm2`, which serves this model correctly on the same hardware.

Environment facts that cost the most time (all now in the install section above and in #12):

- `sglang-kernel` must be the torch-matching build; source builds with
  `-DSGL_KERNEL_V100_ONLY=ON` need the three build fixes from #12.
- No `nvidia-nccl-cu13` in the venv (it shadows torch's cu12 NCCL: "named symbol not found").
- `marlin_v100` needs `MARLIN_V100_SKIP_BF16_COMPAT=1` under CUDA 12.6.
- `--linear-attn-decode-backend tilelang`: Triton lowers `tanh` to PTX `tanh.approx`, sm_75+.
- FlashInfer must be the pinned revision **with** `patches/flashinfer-sm70.patch`, and the JIT
  sampling call must match its `DeviceSamplingFromProb` signature (7 args + `DETERMINISTIC`).
- PLE embedding offload is mandatory on 4x32 GB; on-device OOMs.
